10-Day Expedition Itinerary

Day 1: Departure Airport

Presentation at origin airport 3 hours prior to departure for regular scheduled flight to Buenos Aires. Night aboard.

Day 2: Buenos Aires / Ushuaia & Beagle Channel Cruise

Flight transfer to Ushuaia. Hotel reception. Half-day Beagle Channel navigation past the iconic lighthouse at the end of the world, Isla de Lobos, and Isla de Pájaros. Disembarkation on Bridges Island for an interpretive flora walk and ancient Yámana community shell middens. Onboard snack included.

Day 3: Ushuaia – Vinciguerra Glacier & Iceberg Lagoon Trek

Trek starting from Andorra Valley through peat bogs and century-old forests to Río de la Leche and Laguna de los Témpanos. Equipment fitting and guided ice walk on Vinciguerra Glacier to explore deep crevasses and glacial sinkholes. Packed lunch included (Full Day, Medium difficulty).

Day 4: Ushuaia – 4x4 Lakes Escondido & Fagnano + Kayak + Asado

Crossing the Fuegian Andes via Garibaldi Pass down along Route 3. 1-hour lake kayaking session followed by Land Rover 4x4 driving through off-road forest tracks, mud, and water straight into Lake Fagnano. Traditional Argentine barbecue (asado) outdoors with wine, cheese, and meats. Full Day.

Day 5: Ushuaia / El Calafate – Tierra del Fuego National Park

Exploration through Pipo River valley, Bahía Ensenada, Lake Roca, and Lapataia Bay—the final point of National Route 3 (Pan-American highway from Alaska). (Optional: End of the World Steam Train). Afternoon transfer to airport and flight to El Calafate.

Day 6: El Calafate – Perito Moreno Glacier & Iceberg Cruise

Drive across the steppe into Los Glaciares National Park (UNESCO). Comprehensive exploration of catwalks facing the 5 km wide and 80 m tall glacial wall. Navigation along Rico Lake / Canal de los Témpanos. (Optional: 2–3 hour mini-trekking on the glacier ice with crampons).

Day 7: El Calafate – Estancia Cristina, Upsala 4x4 & Fossil Canyon

Navigation on Lake Argentino among floating icebergs to historic Estancia Cristina. 4x4 ascent to Upsala Glacier viewpoint over the Southern Patagonian Icefield, followed by an interpretive trek through Cañadón de los Fósiles.

Day 8: El Calafate – Free Day

Day at leisure to explore El Calafate, browse craft shops, or visit local nature reserves.

Day 9: El Calafate / Buenos Aires – City Tour

Flight to Buenos Aires. City tour covering historical highlights: Plaza de Mayo, San Telmo, La Boca, and Puerto Madero.

Day 10: Buenos Aires / Flight Home

Transfer to Ezeiza International Airport (EZE) for return flight home. End of expedition services.
